**dropbear labs** is my personal, independent research sandbox. It is where I explore the boundaries between software security, physical computing, and creative interaction designs. 

By building in public, I aim to share the raw mechanics of my engineering sprint cycles—vulnerability research, hardware prototyping, and systems engineering.

---

## Active Research Frontiers

### 1. AI Agent Security & Honeypots (Sucralose)
With the rapid integration of autonomous LLM agents into production codebases, securing these systems against prompt injection, data extraction, and resource hijacking is a critical challenge. 
- **Decoys**: Building fake API environments to intercept malicious scans.
- **Agent Profiling**: Analyzing token usage and request patterns to fingerprint autonomous scrapers.
- **Open Source Security**: Providing lightweight diagnostic configurations for developers.

### 2. Physical Computing & Microcontrollers
Bridging the physical and digital. This vector involves sensor networks, custom PCB setups, LED installations (using NeoPixels and microcontrollers like Arduino, ESP32, and Raspberry Pi Pico), and interactive installations.

### 3. Creative Systems
Designing visual tools, slide shows, and workshops exploring AI's role in creative practice. We probe questions of artistic agency, model training ethics, and interface design.
