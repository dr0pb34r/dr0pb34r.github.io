made for curious people

Come with questions.

You don’t need to be convinced by AI to get something out of these workshops. We’ll make space for the mechanics, the possibilities, and the uncomfortable bits.

Dropbear Labs workshops are independently run by Meg White. Dates and venues will be listed here as they’re confirmed.

What if nothing is booked yet?

Each course page stays here between sessions. Send a waitlist request for the course you’re interested in, or join lab updates for general announcements. A waitlist request is an expression of interest, not a ticket or a guaranteed place.

How do bookings and tickets work?

When a session opens, its course page will show the date, location, price, and a link to book or register. Payment happens through the linked booking service.

Is this an SLQ program?

These are independent Dropbear Labs courses. If you met Meg at an SLQ workshop, you’re in the right place to follow her work. Any future SLQ session will be identified in its event listing.

Can I book something for my team?

Email Meg with your group, idea, and preferred timing. We can work out a useful starting point.
