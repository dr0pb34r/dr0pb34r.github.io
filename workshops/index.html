you don’t need to be convinced by ai to get something out of these workshops. we’ll make space for the mechanics, the possibilities, and the uncomfortable bits.

dropbear labs workshops are independently run by meg white. dates and venues will be listed here as they’re confirmed.

what if nothing is booked yet?

each page stays here between sessions. join the waitlist for the workshop or course you’re interested in, or sign up for postcards. a waitlist signup is an expression of interest, not a ticket or a guaranteed place.

how do bookings and tickets work?

when a session opens, its page will show the date, location, price, and a link to book or register. payment happens through the linked booking service.

can i book something for my team?

email meg with your group, idea, and preferred timing. we can work out a useful starting point.
