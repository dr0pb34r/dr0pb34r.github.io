postcards

{% if site.data.workshops.newsletter_form_url != '' or site.data.workshops.newsletter_embed_url != '' %}

postcards is meg white / dropbear labs’ mailing list. choose workshop and course news, other transmissions, or both. those choices are stored as interest tags in kit, which processes the signup and manages the mailing list. emails are sent to the interests you choose. you can unsubscribe using the link in a newsletter. see kit’s privacy policy for its handling of data.

{% else %}

you can request dropbear labs workshop announcements and occasional lab updates by email. the link opens a draft in your email app. no request is sent until you send that email. meg handles the signup request personally. to stop receiving updates, reply to meg and ask to leave the list.

{% endif %}

workshop and course waitlists

where a course offers a waitlist by email, clicking the link opens a draft in your email app. no request is sent until you send that email. meg uses the contact details and course preference you provide to respond about that course and its availability. a waitlist email does not subscribe you to general lab updates.

waitlist forms are handled by kit. your email is used for news and availability about the offering you select. you can optionally nominate in-person or online delivery and a preferred day. these preferences are stored separately for each offering to help plan future sessions.

confirm your email to join a waitlist. this does not sign you up for postcards, reserve a place, or process payment. you can unsubscribe from the emails. see kit’s privacy policy.

bookings and payments

when a session is available, its booking link takes you to the event’s booking provider. that provider handles registration and payment under its own terms and privacy policy. card details are not entered on this website.

questions or removal

to ask about your information or request removal from a waitlist, email {{ site.data.workshops.contact_email }}.
