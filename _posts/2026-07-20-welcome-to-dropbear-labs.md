Welcome to **dropbear labs**. 

This portal is a real, deep, build-in-public workbench. My goal is to transparently document the design, development, failures, and breakthroughs of my personal engineering explorations. 

## The Philosophy: Why Build in Public?

Much of engineering is hidden behind closed corporate networks or clean, sanitised portfolios. The "messy middle"—the debugging loops, the initial whiteboard notes, the broken builds—is where the real learning happens. 

By exposing my notes, my wiki vault, and my active project telemetry, I want to:
- **Demystify system architecture**: Show how security tools and creative systems are built from scratch.
- **Maintain a living garden**: Maintain my knowledge bases in public, encouraging feedback and collaborations.
- **Accelerate prototyping**: Commit early, test in the wild, and share the findings immediately.

## What is Hosted Here?

- **[[Dropbear Vault|The Wiki]]**: A direct mirror of my local Obsidian vault. It contains raw, interlinked thoughts on AI security, physical computing, and digital design.
- **Field Notes (The Blog)**: Polished summaries and reports on specific project sprints, such as our ongoing [[AI Honeypot - Sucralose]] logging.
- **Workshops**: Direct educational materials, guides, and download vectors for courses and workshops I host (microcontrollers, LED arrays, and AI alignment).

I am glad to have you along for the ride. Let's make some things and break some things.

*— Meaghan*
